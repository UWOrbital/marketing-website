## The project timeline on the Mission page

Image: `https://static.wixstatic.com/media/6a67b4_34779a5f43cd4a4c97977fb516357365~mv2.jpg`

Six milestones on one line. The spelling "CubSat" is in the original.

| Date | Milestone | Text |
|---|---|---|
| May 2024 | Research & CSDC Kickoff | Majority of the research and technical documentation has been done over the summer 2024. CSDC-7 officially started in Sep 2024. |
| Oct 2024 | FlatSat Prototyping | The FlatSat is a high fidelity electrical and functional representation of our CubSat bus. The first prototype. |
| Apr 2025 | Subsystem Construction | Development of each subsystem, meaning that each subsystem would be built and tested as it was going into space. |
| Oct 2025 | Final Assembly | Assembling subsystem pieces into a CubSat. Performing vibration, thermal testing with our partners to prepare for competition. |
| Mar 2026 | Vibration & Environmental Testing | Official evaluation part of CSDC. CubSat will undergo testing to simulate the forces of launch on the CubSat to select the winner. |
| Jun 2026 | Winner Announced & Gets Launched | The winner of CSDC is going to be awarded money for the launch and we aspire to take no less than the first place. The launch date is TBD. |

Two facts come only from this image:

1. The team is now in **CSDC-7**, which started in September 2024.
2. The competition ends in **June 2026**. The launch date is not set.
