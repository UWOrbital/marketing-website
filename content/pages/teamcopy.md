# Our Team

## Meet the S23 Team

Our team brings together students from various programs and faculties to pursue their interest in the space industry through specialized and unique opportunities. Students from the University of Waterloo get to apply skills they have learned and grow their expertise and knowledge through mentorship and have a safe place to explore their potential. Members of UW Orbital are great team players and problem solvers, love to tackle challenges, show passion and commitment to their work, have a great sense of humor, and want to grow their horizons as much as they can!
