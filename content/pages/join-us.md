## Why Join Us?

We're building a space community at the University of Waterloo, and our mission is to launch the university's first satellite in orbit to do so. If you're interested in building tech that will actually make it to space right during your undergraduate degree, you've come to the right place. Plus, you can do it all while gaining valuable skills in mechanical, electrical, and software engineering.

Learn more about [our subteams here](https://www.uworbital.com/copy-of-team)
