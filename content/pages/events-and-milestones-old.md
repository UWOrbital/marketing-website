# Events & Milestones

Here are a few recent events and milestones that Orbital has met!

![20220907_154926.jpg](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/6a67b4_a24b63f274224d9399f4f1286d67c4c5~mv2.jpg)

## CDR (Critical Design Review)

A few of our members previously went to Ottawa to present an overview of our satellite design plans to a panel of judges for a mid-point design review!

![20221026_174744.jpg](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/6a67b4_2e1b38c55d1145938d56abefaed9c490~mv2.jpg)

## Christie Digital Satellite Chassis Vibration Testing

The Mechanical team went to Christie Digital to test our satellite chassis that underwent an aggressive vibration testing and our cube satellite passed the vibration test not once, but twice!!

## Fall 2022 student design showcase

We got the chance to present to many students about our team at the Student Design Showcase!

## SDC Volleyball tournament

UW Orbital participated in an SDC volleyball tournament as a fun social event~
